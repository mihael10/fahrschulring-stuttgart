# Competitor research: Stuttgart driving schools (for Fahrschulring Stuttgart)

Research date: 2026-10-09. Method: about 33 WebSearch queries (US-based search with result summaries). I could not fetch any competitor site directly (WebFetch/curl blocked), and the session's web-search budget ran out before every candidate could be checked (see Limitations).

Labels used:
- **FACT**: stated in a cited source (the source may still be outdated).
- **OBSERVATION**: a pattern seen in search results or snippets (titles, URLs, what turns up for a query).
- **INFERENCE**: my interpretation.

**Ratings caveat.** I could not open any Google Maps profile. Every "Google" figure below comes from a third-party page that quotes Google, or from a combined score on an aggregator. The source is named each time. Treat all figures as approximate and as of the date the source was crawled, which is unknown and often months old.

- **Location (FACT):** Rotebühlstraße 127B, 70178 Stuttgart-West. Directories label it either Mitte or West. Das Örtliche also lists Schloßstraße 57, 70176. Branches: 1, possibly 2.

- **INFERENCE:** this is the most direct rival. It is a few minutes from Hegelplatz, has a similar rating (4.9, about 300 reviews), and also has a simulator. It beats Fahrschulring on price transparency and press visibility, since it appears in the t-online article.

## 4. Analysis

### A) What competitors do better than Fahrschulring
1. **Public prices.** Fun S Drive, Peter's, Wengert and Sieber publish lesson prices, and the first three are cited by t-online. Fahrschulring is missing from press and comparison coverage. (FACT and INFERENCE)
2. **Intensive-course landing pages.** Fun S Drive ("10 Werktage", "8 Werktage" for motorcycle), Lutz (7 and 12 working days), Sieber (in the homepage title), Campos ("10 Tage") and Schille/PS (holiday courses) all have them. (FACT)
3. **Per-class pages.** Lutz has a dedicated truck page, Peter's and M.T.V. have class pages, and Fun S Drive has an online truck and bus registration page. (OBSERVATION)
4. **Location and district pages.** Lutz, Baumann and Fun S Drive have per-location pages, and Thomas' at least targets districts in its title. (OBSERVATION)
5. **English and foreign-licence content.** Maier (a separate English domain), stuttgartexpats, Twentyfive (`/en`), and Kesmez (5-language registration). (FACT)
6. **Review-volume momentum.** Schille has about 430–590 Google reviews (aggregators) against Fahrschulring's 325. Schille also runs a referral discount. (FACT)
7. **Placement in aggregator "top lists"** (section 3).

### B) What Fahrschulring does better
1. **Rating with volume:** 4.9 from 325 (Google, 2026-10-02, as given in the brief).
   - Only Peter's (about 4.9 from about 300) and Schille (about 4.9 from more than 400) match or exceed this.
   - Lutz (about 4.6–4.7), PS (about 4.3) and Campos (4.69 combined) are lower. (FACT and INFERENCE)
2. **Full range of classes at one central address, including T and L.**
   - Only Campos (spread over several sites) and Lutz (with a separate truck site in Wangen) cover car, motorcycle, truck and bus in Stuttgart.
   - No competitor found combines the full bus range (D1E, DE) with a city-centre location. (INFERENCE)
3. **Central location (Hegelplatz/Berliner Platz).** It is close to public transport and the city centre. Only PS (Neue Brücke), Lutz (Sophienstr.) and Peter's (Rotebühlstr.) are comparably central. (INFERENCE)
4. **EV fleet (VW ID.3, MG4, Tesla Model S).**
   - No competitor found advertises named EV models.
   - The only EV mention found is the generic "Pkw-Führerschein auf einem Elektroauto" at "Mit Sicherheit ans Ziel". (OBSERVATION)
5. **Simulator.**
   - Only Peter's, Lutz (truck simulator) and Baumann (Vaihingen and Echterdingen, outside the centre) advertise one.
   - Fahrschulring would be one of only a few in the centre with one. (OBSERVATION)
6. **50+ years in business.** Schille says about 20 years, and Thomas' Vaihingen branch dates from 2013. No competitor in the results claims a longer history. (OBSERVATION)

### C) What no competitor does well
- **Clear B197 and automatic explanations** for a Stuttgart audience. B197 is listed by many schools but explained by none in their snippets. (OBSERVATION)
- **EV-specific training content**, for example what learning on an ID.3 or Tesla means for the B197 versus B78 choice.
- **A transparent cost calculator or worked example.** Only Wengert publishes its own example total.
- **Pass-rate data.** Peter's claims a "hohe Erstbestehensquote" without a number. No school found publishes a figure.
- **Fear of driving ("Fahrangst"), refresher lessons for returning drivers, and confidence lessons for adults.** Peter's lists "Auffrischung" without any depth. No school found has a dedicated page.
- **Truck and bus career content:** professional-driver qualification (BKF), Bildungsgutschein funding, employer-paid licences. Lutz and Kesmez list BKF but give no guidance on funding.
- **Waiting times and how fast you can start.** None of the schools found states them.

### D) High-commercial-intent opportunities (INFERENCE)
1. "Führerschein Kosten Stuttgart" / "Fahrschule Stuttgart Preise". Press and comparison sites already rank for these queries. Publishing real prices could also get Fahrschulring into the next t-online-style comparison.
2. "LKW Führerschein Stuttgart" / "Busführerschein Stuttgart" / "C1/CE/D Führerschein Stuttgart". Few competitors, and the queries have high value per customer.
3. "Intensivkurs Führerschein Stuttgart" / "Ferienkurs". Many competitors here, but the pages are thin.
4. "Automatik Führerschein Stuttgart" / "B197 Stuttgart" / "Führerschein Elektroauto Stuttgart". This is where the EV fleet differentiates.
5. "Fahrschule Stuttgart West / Mitte / Nord", "Fahrschule Hegelplatz / Berliner Platz". Thomas' and Peter's are the neighbourhood rivals.
6. "Motorradführerschein Stuttgart", "A2 auf A", "B196". The motorcycle season brings a spring demand peak.
7. "Führerschein umschreiben Stuttgart" / "driving licence Stuttgart English". Proven demand (Maier, Expats, Twentyfive).

## 5. Limitations
- **About 33 searches**, then the session's WebSearch budget (200 per turn, shared across agents) ran out.
  - I did not check individually: Fahrschule Kopp, Bartsch, Walz, Lehner, Bühler, F-1, Pfeiffer, Gruber, Holder, DEKRA/TÜV/ADAC.
  - Not obtained: Campos' and Fun S Drive's Google figures on their own.
- **No direct page fetches.** Content, title and H1 notes come from search snippets and summaries. H1s could not be seen; only titles and URLs.
- **Ratings come from third-party aggregators** that quote Google or combine platforms, with unknown crawl dates (some pages are about 2 years old). Counts for the same school vary by up to 40% between sources.
- **Directory data is often stale or conflicting** (addresses, hours, branch status). City-Fahrschule's liquidation status comes from the commercial register via northdata (2022).
- **Search is US-based**, so the German result rankings may not match what users in Stuttgart see.
