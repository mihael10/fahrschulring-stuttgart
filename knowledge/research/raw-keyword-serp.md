# Local SEO keyword / SERP research: Fahrschulring Stuttgart

Research date: 2026-10-09. Business: Fahrschulring Stuttgart GmbH, Hegelstraße 48, 70174 Stuttgart (Stuttgart-West, at the edge of Mitte), fahrschulring.de.

## 0. Method and limitations (read first)

- **FACT:** The only search tool available was `WebSearch`, a US-based web search that returns ranked links plus a model-written summary. It is **not Google**. WebFetch/curl were blocked, so no result page was opened directly. Everything below comes from result titles, URLs and the summaries the tool produced.
- **FACT: none of the Google SERP features could be observed:** Local Pack/Map Pack, People Also Ask, featured snippets, AI Overviews, related searches, ads, review stars, sitelinks. "Recurring questions" below are a **proxy**: topics and questions that kept appearing in result titles and snippets.
- **FACT:** The positions recorded are the **order of links returned by this tool**, not Google rankings. No search volumes were available, and none are given.
- **OBSERVATION:** `standard` mode returned poor results for this niche: StepStone job pages, dasoertliche/oeffnungszeitenbuch, an AI-spam page (biketrailspfannenstiel.ch), reviewhero.io, and off-topic results for B197/B78/BF17 (an F1 car, WWII B-17 bombers). `extended` mode returned real competitor sites and aggregators. Most queries were therefore re-run in `extended`, and the tables use the extended results where both exist.
- **FACT:** The shared web-search budget (200 calls/turn) ran out before these were run: **Busführerschein Stuttgart, Fahrschule Stuttgart West, Fahrschule Stuttgart englisch** (and the extended re-run of "Fahrschule in Stuttgart"; the standard run was recorded). Partial evidence for "englisch" appears in other queries (see §1.6).
- Query variants: some queries were made more specific to get past ambiguous results: "Klasse A Stuttgart" → "Führerschein Klasse A Stuttgart Fahrschule"; "A1/A2 Stuttgart" → "A1/A2 Führerschein Stuttgart"; "AM Stuttgart" → "AM Führerschein Roller Stuttgart"; "B78 Stuttgart" → "B78 Automatik Führerschein Stuttgart Fahrschule"; "B197 Stuttgart" → "B197 Führerschein Stuttgart Fahrschule"; "BF17 Stuttgart" → "BF17 begleitetes Fahren Fahrschule Stuttgart"; "B96 Stuttgart" → "B96 Stuttgart Anhänger Fahrschule"; "Fahrschulring Bewertungen" → also "Fahrschulring Stuttgart Bewertungen"; "Auffrischungsfahrten" → also "Auffrischungsstunden Fahrschule Stuttgart".

Labels: **FACT** = seen directly in the result list or tool output. **OBSERVATION** = a pattern across results. **INFERENCE** = my interpretation or recommendation.

Page-type codes: **C-H** competitor homepage, **C-L** competitor landing/class page, **AGG** driving-school aggregator (clickclickdrive, fahrschulen.de, drivolino, infahrschulen, fahrschulekosten, 123fahrschule), **DIR** generic directory (gelbeseiten, dasoertliche, 11880, goyellow, cylex, oeffnungszeitenbuch, meinestadt, golocal, werkenntdenbesten, listando, city-map), **GOV** government (stuttgart.de, service-bw.de, bmv.de), **PRESS** news, **INFO** third-party info/guide, **JOB** job board, **SPAM** low-quality/AI filler.
