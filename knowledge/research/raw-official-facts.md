## 1. Führerscheinreform ("Bezahlbarer Führerschein"), status Oct 2026

**Bottom line: NOT in force. Bill is in the Bundestag (committee stage). Current rules apply unchanged.**

- Still pending: 2nd/3rd reading, Bundesrat. dpa 9 Oct 2026: reform "muss noch vom Bundestag verabschiedet werden; auch der Bundesrat muss zustimmen". https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/bestenfalls-bis-zu-1000-euro-bilger-beziffert-einsparungen-beim-fuhrerschein-durch-reform-16143618.html ; https://www.nw.de/nachrichten/politik/24436693_Verkehrsminister-Bilger-Fuehrerschein-koennte-bis-zu-1.000-Euro-guenstiger-werden.html (both 2026-10-09). **High**

- Draft staging reported: training rules 1 Jan 2027, exam changes + law 1 Jul 2027; transitional rule (§ 15 of new Fahrschulausbildungs-Verordnung draft): training begun before 1 Jan 2027 may be finished under old rules. Reported by dpa summary / fahrschul blogs (funsdrive.de, mannis-fahrschule.com). **Medium-Low** — not verified in the official text.
- **Minister change:** Patrick Schnieder was Federal Transport Minister until 29 Jul 2026; successor **Steffen Bilger** (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Schnieder ; dpa articles of 9 Oct 2026 call Bilger "Verkehrsminister"). **Medium** (Wikipedia + dpa consistent; the reason for the change was not researched). → Website copy should not say "Minister Schnieder's reform" in present tense.

- BMV: current average cost of Klasse B ≈ **3.400 €** (Bundesregierung/BMV, May 2026; repeated by dpa 9 Oct 2026). **High**

### Planned content (draft, may still change)
- **Theory:** no more mandatory classroom attendance; driving schools choose classroom, online or mixed; asynchronous formats explicitly allowed; many requirements for classrooms removed. ADAC (May 2026) https://www.adac.de/news/verkehr-wie-der-fuehrerschein-guenstiger-werden-soll/ ; Bundesregierung (May 2026). **High** (as plan)
- Limit of max 2 theory units (90 min) per day to be dropped (intensive courses). dvpi.de https://dvpi.de/news/fahrausbildung-reform-2026-aenderungen.html. **Medium-Low**
- **Question catalogue:** currently **1,169** questions (since 1 Apr 2025, AUTO BILD https://www.autobild.de/artikel/fuehrerschein-neue-theoriefragen-ab-1.-april-28010643.html); to be cut by about **one third**; each question to count 1 point flat. ADAC May 2026; ZDF https://www.zdfheute.de/politik/deutschland/fuehrerschein-reform-plaene-ueberblick-schnieder-100.html. **High** (as plan)
- **Sonderfahrten:** Bundestag: no fixed number anymore (aligning with EU law); Überland/Autobahn/darkness driving stays mandatory, scope per individual training level, complete omission excluded. https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw39-de-fahrlehrergesetz-1211282. **High**. Fahrschul blogs claim "minimum 3 (1 each)" — contradicted; treat as **Low/unverified**.
- **Simulators:** simulator training generally permitted as supplement; for B, manual-gearbox practice possible virtually. ADAC May 2026; auto-motor-und-sport https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/verkehr/simulator-statt-strasse-fuehrerscheinreform-bringt-wandel/. **Medium-High** (as plan). Does not replace practical lessons wholesale (fahrschul sources). **Medium**
- **Practical exam:** total duration **40 min instead of currently 55 min**, of which **25 min driving time** (EU minimum). dpa 9 Oct 2026 (sources above). **High** (as plan)
- **Experimentation clause / Laienausbildung:** new § 2e FahrlG (draft): authority-approved practice driving on public roads with close persons (Austrian model), supplementing (not replacing) driving school. Drs. 21/7404; ADAC May 2026. **High** (as plan). Details (5-year pilot, Länder decide, 6 lessons first, companion ≥ 7 years licence) from secondary sources only → **Low**.
- **Transparency:** prices and pass rates of all driving schools to be published in the Mobilithek. Bundesregierung May 2026. **High** (as plan)
- Existing licences unaffected. ADAC. **High**

## 2. B197 / B78
- B197: Klasse B exam on automatic, plus ≥ **10 lessons à 45 min on manual** + **≥ 15-min test drive** on manual with the instructor (inside and outside built-up areas); driving school issues certificate; no separate exam. ADAC https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/erwerb/b197-fuehrerschein/ ; Berlin LABO https://www.berlin.de/labo/mobilitaet/fahrerlaubnisse-personen-und-gueterbefoerderung/aktuelles/artikel.1445531.php ; § 17a FeV. (in force since 1 Apr 2021 — from memory, not re-verified in this search). **High**
- Legal basis § 17a FeV (key number 197) / FahrschAusbO. https://lxgesetze.de/fev/17a. **High**
- Holders of B78 (automatic only) can get 197 added later without a new full exam by doing the manual training + test drive and presenting proof to the licence office. bussgeldkatalog.org https://www.bussgeldkatalog.org/b197-fuehrerschein/. **Medium**
- Extending to BE, C1, C... with 197 leads to automatic restriction on those classes. bussgeldkatalog.org. **Medium**
- B78 still exists (automatic-only). **Medium**. Reform plan: manual practice possible on simulator for B197 (see §1). **Medium**

## 3. BF17
- Min age 17 (§ 10 FeV + § 48a FeV) https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/__10.html. **High**
- Companion: ≥ **30 years**, ≥ **5 years** uninterrupted Klasse B, max **1 point** in FAER at time of application, < 0.5‰ / no drugs, named in the Prüfungsbescheinigung (unlimited number, can be added later). ADAC https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/erwerb/begleitetes-fahren/ ; ACE https://www.ace.de/ratgeber/verkehrsrecht/fuehrerschein/begleitetes-fahren/. **High**
- Valid only in Germany (and Austria) until 18. ADAC. **Medium-High**
- Stuttgart fee BF17 application: 8.70 € + 11 € per companion (stuttgart.de "from 17" page https://www.stuttgart.de/en/organigramm/leistungen/fuehrerschein-beantragen-ab-17-jahre, as excerpted). **Medium** (verify on page).
- EU Directive (EU) 2025/2205 (in force 25 Nov 2025, Germany has until ~2029 to transpose) makes accompanied driving at 17 EU-wide; ACE https://www.ace.de/ratgeber/verkehrsrecht/fuehrerschein/fuehrerscheinrichtlinie/ ; EP https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/de/press-room/20250324IPR27462/verkehrssicherheit-einigung-uber-reform-der-eu-fuhrerscheinregeln. **Medium-High**. No enacted change to German companion rules found; no BF16 decision found. **Medium** (absence of evidence).

## 4. B196 / B96
- B196: ≥ **25 years**, ≥ **5 years** Klasse B, training ≥ 9 units à 90 min (**4 theory + 5 practical**), **no exam**, register within 1 year of training; bikes ≤ 125 cm³, ≤ 11 kW, ≤ 0.1 kW/kg; **Germany only**; does not grant A1. Hamburg https://www.hamburg.de/service/info/11392141/n0/ ; LRA Schwäbisch Hall PDF https://www.LRASHA.de/fileadmin/Dateien/Dateien/Formulare_A_-_Z/Fuehrerscheine/Schluesselzahl_B196.pdf ; ADAC. **High**
- Deutsche Verkehrswacht demanded abolition of B196 in current form (Jul 2026); not decided. motorradundreisen.de https://www.motorradundreisen.de/szene/forderung-b196-abgeschafft/9770/. **Medium**
- B96: car + trailer > 750 kg, combination > 3,500 kg up to 4,250 kg; min age 18 (17 with BF17); **≥ 7 hours** training at a driving school, no exam; EU-valid. Berlin LABO https://www.berlin.de/labo/mobilitaet/fahrerlaubnisse-personen-und-gueterbefoerderung/aktuelles/artikel.1445537.php. **High**

- **EU/EEA licences**: no conversion needed (exchange optional). Stuttgart EU exchange page https://www.stuttgart.de/en/organigramm/leistungen/fuehrerschein-umtausch-in-eu-fuehrerschein-beantragen. **Medium-High**
- **Anlage 11 FeV states**: conversion without exams (some only partially, e.g. certain US states require theory only; scope may be class B only); all other third countries: **theory + practical exam** (no mandatory driving school lessons — from memory, Medium). ## 9. Application requirements & training (current law)
- Documents (A, A1, A2, B, BE, AM, L, T): ID/passport, biometric photo, eye test certificate (amtlich anerkannte Sehteststelle, ≤ 2 years old), proof of first aid, driving school registration. ADAC https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/erwerb/fuehrerschein-antrag-noetige-papiere/ ; stuttgart.de Ersterteilung page. **High**. Meldebescheinigung: not listed by ADAC/Stuttgart for Germans (ID suffices) — **Medium**.
- First aid: ≥ **9 UE à 45 min** (§ 19 FeV), all classes. ADAC/DRK. **High**. Some authorities reject online-only courses (Köln) — Stuttgart not checked. **Low**
- Application earliest 6 months before min age; application expires if theory exam not taken within 12 months of Prüfauftrag. Karlsruhe service page https://web1.karlsruhe.de/service/Buergerdienste/leistung.php?id=6022545. **Medium-High**
- Probezeit 2 years. **High**
- Theory Klasse B: **12 double lessons Grundstoff + 2 Zusatzstoff = 14 × 90 min** (Erweiterung: 6 + 2). ADAC https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/erwerb/ausbildung/. **High** — still current (reform not in force).
- Sonderfahrten B: **12 × 45 min = 5 Überland, 4 Autobahn, 3 Dunkelheit**; no minimum of normal lessons. ADAC; Anlage 4 FahrschAusbO. **High** — still current.
- Theory exam B: 30 questions, max 10 error points (no two 5-point errors). ADAC theory page https://www.adac.de/verkehr/rund-um-den-fuehrerschein/erwerb/theoretische-fuehrerscheinpruefung/. **High**
- Practical exam B: currently 55 min (dpa Oct 2026); some guides say 45 min with ~25 min driving. **Medium** — sources conflict; avoid exact minutes or say "rund eine Stunde inkl. Vor-/Nachbereitung".
