## A. What competitors do better (FACT/OBS)

1. Published prices (Fun S Drive, Peter's, Sieber, Wengert) → cited by
   t-online and price aggregators; Fahrschulring is absent from all.
2. Intensive-course landing pages (Fun S Drive 10/8 Werktage, Lutz 7/12,
   Sieber, Campos, Schille).
3. One page per class; location pages (Lutz, Baumann, Fun S Drive).
4. English/foreign-licence content (Maier, Twentyfive, Kesmez, stuttgartexpats).
5. Review-volume momentum + referral programme (Schille).
6. Presence in aggregator "best of" lists (threebestrated, dasoertliche Top,
   infahrschulen, listando, werkenntdenbesten) — Fahrschulring in none.

## B. What Fahrschulring does better (FACT)

1. 4.9★ with 325 reviews (2026-10-02) — only Peter's (~300) and Schille
   (>400) are comparable; most central rivals are lower.
2. Full range AM → DE + T/L **at one central address** — only Campos (multi-
   site) and Lutz (truck site in Wangen) compare; no rival combines full bus
   range with a city-centre location.
3. Central location (Hegelstraße, Mitte/West border).
4. Named EV fleet (ID.3, MG4, Tesla Model S) — no rival advertises models.
5. Simulator in the centre (only Peter's, Lutz-truck, Baumann-Vaihingen have one).
6. 50+ years (no rival claims more; Horlacher "since 1925" is the exception,
   in Möhringen).

## C. What nobody does well (OBS)

B197 vs B78 explained for Stuttgart · EV-specific training content · honest
cost breakdown (only Wengert has a worked example) · pass-rate data ·
Fahrangst/Auffrischung depth · truck-licence funding guidance · waiting
times / how fast you can start · reform status explained.
