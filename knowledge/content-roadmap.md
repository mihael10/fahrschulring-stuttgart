description with NAP + link) and embed on the relevant page:

performance and the privacy policy simple (add a section to Datenschutz
when embedding).

## Image strategy

Keep all current images; when new photos are shot: descriptive file names
(`fahrschulring-stuttgart-vw-id3-elektro.webp`), ≤ 1600 px, `.webp`, alt
written from the picture, per-vehicle photos for the unmapped fleet entries
(Polo, X1, X2, Tesla, Sprinter, Actros, Setra, Tuono, Hornet) and higher-
resolution team portraits (current ones are 200 px).
